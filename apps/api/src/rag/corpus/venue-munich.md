## Allianz Arena (Munich) — Bag Policy

Only bags up to **25cm × 20cm × 10cm** (roughly A5 size) are permitted inside Allianz Arena. Backpacks and bags exceeding these dimensions are **not allowed** and there is **no bag storage** at the stadium on Champions League matchdays. Supporters arriving with oversized bags will be refused entry at the turnstiles and must return the items off-site.

## Allianz Arena — Gate Entry and Digital Tickets

Entry is via mobile digital ticket only; screenshots are not accepted. If a digital ticket fails to scan at the gate:

1. Ensure screen brightness is at maximum and the UEFA wallet app is open on the live ticket.
2. Move to the clearly marked **Ticket Resolution Point** beside Gate C (East).
3. Staff can re-issue a scannable pass after verifying Supporter ID and PIN.

If the resolution point cannot restore entry within matchday service time, the case must be escalated to the stadium duty officer. Gate C opens **two hours before kickoff**.

## Allianz Arena — Accessibility

Wheelchair-accessible entry is at Gate A (North). Accessible hospitality guests should contact the hospitality desk on arrival; step-free routes to all VIP boxes are available.
