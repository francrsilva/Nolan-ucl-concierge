## Ticket Transfer Policy

Active match tickets may be transferred to another registered UEFA supporter free of charge via the mobile pass system. Transfers are subject to the following hard rules:

- Transfers close **3 hours before kickoff**. After this cutoff, no digital transfer is possible and the supporter must visit the stadium box office in person with photo ID.
- Each ticket may be transferred **at most once** (anti-scalping / anti-touting policy). A ticket that has already been transferred cannot be transferred again.
- Only the current, verified ticket holder may initiate a transfer.
- Hospitality (VIP) passes are transferable under the same window but the recipient must complete a separate ID verification at the hospitality desk.

## Refund and Cancellation Policy

Match tickets are **non-refundable** once issued, except where a fixture is cancelled or abandoned by UEFA. If a match is postponed, tickets remain valid for the rescheduled date automatically. Travel and hospitality packages are governed by the separate UEFA Official Travel terms and may carry their own cancellation schedule.

## UEFA Champions League Pass — Subscription Terms

The UEFA Champions League Pass is a monthly streaming and club-membership subscription billed at €19.99/month. Supporters may pause, cancel, or modify the subscription at any time from their account.

- **Cancellation** takes effect at the end of the current billing period; access continues until then.
- **Pause**: eligible supporters may pause the subscription until the start of the next season's group stage instead of cancelling, with no charges during the pause.
- **Loyalty retention**: a one-time 50% elimination discount may be offered per season to eligible supporters whose supported club has been knocked out.
- A supporter who insists on cancelling must always be allowed to cancel immediately; retention offers are optional and must never be forced.

## Prohibited Items (All Venues)

The following are prohibited inside all UEFA Champions League stadiums: glass bottles, cans, flares/pyrotechnics, large flags with poles exceeding 1 metre, laptops, professional cameras with detachable lenses, and any bag larger than an A4 sheet (see venue-specific bag policy for exact dimensions).

## Identity Verification

Before any account-affecting or ticket-affecting action, the supporter's identity must be verified using their UEFA Supporter ID and 4-digit PIN. High-value hospitality actions may require an additional ID check at the venue.
