## Santiago Bernabéu (Madrid) — Bag Policy

Bags up to A4 size (**30cm × 21cm**) are permitted. Limited paid bag storage is available at the North-West corner up to 90 minutes before kickoff, subject to capacity. Oversized luggage and suitcases are never admitted.

## Santiago Bernabéu — Gate Entry and Digital Tickets

The Bernabéu uses NFC mobile ticketing. For a ticket that will not scan:

1. Open the live pass in the UEFA wallet and hold the phone flat against the NFC reader.
2. Proceed to the **Fan Services booth at Gate 30** for re-issue after ID + PIN verification.
3. Persistent failures on matchday are escalated to the stadium duty officer for manual admission.

Turnstiles open **90 minutes before kickoff**. VIP hospitality guests use the dedicated entrance on Avenida de Concha Espina.

## Santiago Bernabéu — Hospitality

Platinum and hospitality ticket holders have access to the premium lounges from three hours before kickoff. A separate ID check applies to transferred hospitality passes at the hospitality reception.
